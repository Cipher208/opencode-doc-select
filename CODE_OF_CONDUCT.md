# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be decent. Assume the other person is competent and is trying to help, including
when they are wrong — and especially when you are the one who is wrong.

## Expected behaviour

- Review the code, not the person. "This loses the last line" beats "you dropped
  a line".
- State your evidence. If a claim is a guess, say so; if it is measured, show
  the measurement.
- Accept correction without ceremony, and correct your own work when it turns out
  to be wrong.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, sustained disruption, publishing anyone's private
information, and dismissing someone's work on the basis of who they are.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, commits, and any other space where someone
is representing this project.

## Enforcement

Report it through a GitHub Security Advisory on this repository, or contact the
maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially and reviewed before any
public action is taken.
